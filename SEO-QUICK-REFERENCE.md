## 💡 Content Writing Tips

### Always Include:
1. **Main keyword in H1** (exactly once)
2. **2-3 secondary keywords in H2s**
3. **Core keywords in first paragraph** (naturally)
4. **Internal links** (3-5 per page)
5. **Call-to-action** with "book ride online" or "book now"

### Keyword Density
- Main keyword: 1-2% of content
- Secondary keywords: 0.5-1% each
- Natural language first, SEO second

### Example Opening Paragraph:
```
Experience seamless [airport transfers] with our professional [chauffeur service]. 
Our [luxury travel marketplace] connects you with [licensed chauffeurs] and 
[premium vehicles] for all your [private transfers] and [day trips]. 
[Book ride online] and compare prices from verified providers.
```
