# ✅ Advanced Features Complete!

## 🎉 **What's New**

### **1. 📅 Calendar View**
**URL:** `http://localhost:10003/wp-admin/admin.php?page=gtub-calendar`

**Features:**
- ✅ **FullCalendar Integration** - Professional calendar interface
- ✅ **Multiple Views** - Month, Week, Day, List views
- ✅ **Color-Coded Events** - Events colored by status
- ✅ **Click to View** - Click any booking to see details in a modal
- ✅ **Responsive** - Works on all devices
- ✅ **Real-Time Data** - Loads bookings dynamically

**Color Legend:**
- 🟡 **Yellow** - Pending
- 🔵 **Blue** - Confirmed
- 🟢 **Green** - Assigned
- 🔵 **Dark Blue** - In Progress
- ⚫ **Gray** - Completed

**How to Use:**
1. Go to: `Unified Bookings → Calendar`
2. Navigate months with prev/next buttons
3. Switch views (Month/Week/Day/List)
4. Click any booking to see details
5. Click "View Details" to go to full booking page

---

### **2. 📊 Reports & Analytics**
**URL:** `http://localhost:10003/wp-admin/admin.php?page=gtub-reports`

**Features:**
- ✅ **Date Range Filter** - Filter by custom date range
- ✅ **Key Metrics** - Total bookings, revenue, completed, avg. value
- ✅ **Revenue by Source** - Doughnut chart (CHBS, JetBooking, Manual, Email)
- ✅ **Bookings by Status** - Pie chart (Pending, Confirmed, etc.)
- ✅ **Daily Trends** - Line chart showing bookings & revenue over time
- ✅ **Top Drivers** - Leaderboard with trips and revenue
- ✅ **CSV Export** - Download full booking data

**Metrics Displayed:**
1. **Total Bookings** - Count of all bookings in date range
2. **Total Revenue** - Sum of all paid bookings
3. **Completed Bookings** - Count of completed trips
4. **Avg. Booking Value** - Average revenue per booking

**Charts:**
1. **Revenue by Source** - See which source generates most revenue
2. **Bookings by Status** - Distribution of booking statuses
3. **Daily Bookings & Revenue** - Trend analysis over time

**Top Drivers Table:**
- Driver name
- Total trips
- Total revenue
- Average per trip

**Export:**
- Click "Export CSV" to download all bookings
- Includes: Booking #, Source, Customer, Locations, Date, Status, Payment, Price

### **4. 📥 Export Functionality**
**Available in:** Reports Page

**Features:**
- ✅ **CSV Export** - Download bookings as CSV
- ✅ **Date Range** - Export specific date range
- ✅ **All Fields** - Includes all booking data

**Exported Fields:**

- Pickup & Dropoff Locations
- Pickup Date
- Passengers
- Vehicle Type

**How to Export:**
